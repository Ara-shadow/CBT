# Student and parent guide

## For students

### Logging in

1. Open the school's URL in any browser (ask your teacher for the link)
2. Log in with your username and password
3. You land on your dashboard

### Taking an exam

1. Your dashboard shows **Available exams** with a **Start** button
2. Click **Start**
3. The exam opens with a countdown timer at the top

**In the exam:**

- Click an option, or press **A–E** on the keyboard
- Press **→** or **←** to move between questions
- Press **F** to flag a question for review
- The right-hand panel shows which questions are answered
- Click **Submit exam** when done (or it submits automatically when time runs out)

**Important rules:**

- **Do not open another browser tab or window.** The system will
  immediately log you out and your answers will not be saved.
- You cannot retake an exam once you've submitted it.

### Viewing your results

1. On your dashboard, look for **My results**
2. Published reports appear there with a **View** button
3. Click **View** to open and print your report

Your teachers will tell you when results are available.

## For parents

You can check your child's results in two ways:

### Option 1 — Private link (no login)

Your child's school sends you a link by WhatsApp or SMS. It looks like:

    https://yourschool.com/report.html?t=a8f3k2j9x7...

Just tap it. You'll see your child's report card.

**The link is private to you** — don't forward it. It expires after
30 days. If you need it again, ask the school for a new one.

### Option 2 — Parent login

If the school gave you a username and password:

1. Open the school's URL
2. Log in
3. You'll see your children listed
4. Click **View** on any published report to open it

### Printing a report

1. Open the report
2. Press **Ctrl + P** (Windows) or **Cmd + P** (Mac)
3. Choose your printer, or **Save as PDF**
4. The layout is already sized for A4 paper

### If a link doesn't work

- **"This link has expired"** — the 30 days are up. Ask the school for a new one.
- **"This link has been revoked"** — the school turned it off. Contact them.
- **"This link is not valid"** — you may have typed it wrong, or it was
  copied incorrectly. Ask the school to resend.

### Questions about the report

Contact your child's class teacher or the school office. The system
does not answer questions — only the school can.

## For both

- **Do not share your login** with anyone
- **Do not forward report links** — they are private to your family
- **If you think someone else has your password**, tell the school immediately
